Digital ANDON user manual

When a line has a problem, the GAP leader calls ANDON from a phone. The responsible department is alerted at once (phone alert and KakaoTalk), acknowledges, acts and closes it. The dashboard shows the problem line in place on the plant map.

First time

1. Put the icon on your phone

Nothing to install from a store: add the page to the home screen and use it like an app. The icon is navy with a red / amber / green signal and the word “ANDON”.

Android · Chrome

  1. Open the address above (or the QR code) in Chrome.
  2. Menu ⋮ → Add to home screen (or Install app).
  3. Tap Add.

iPhone · Safari

  1. Open the address in Safari.
  2. Share (□↑) → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Check the name “ANDON” and tap Add.

2. Log in · password

  1. Log in
    Login ID: your employee number (or the e-mail you were given); password: the temporary password from the system owner, Young-Hwan OH (QC). The login is kept on the device.
  2. Change the password
    Top right name ⚙ → My account → Change password (8+ characters, letters and numbers).
  3. No account?
    Accounts are created by the system owner; please ask Young-Hwan OH (QC).
Roles are set by the administrator. Only GAP leaders and supervisors can call ANDON; responders act on the ANDON of their department; visitors and the big screen only view.

3. Alerts (responders)

  1. My account → Turn on alerts on this device → Allow
    The phone then rings and vibrates for every ANDON of your department. iPhone: open ANDON from the home-screen icon first (iOS 16.4+).
  2. Optional: Link KakaoTalk alerts
    The call also arrives in your own KakaoTalk ‘chat with myself’ (silent), with a link to the ANDON.
GAP leader · supervisor

4. Call ANDON

An operator who cannot solve a problem calls the GAP leader; the GAP leader calls the department. Usually 2–3 taps.

  1. Tap the line
    “My lines” come first (a GAP leader also sees the other lines of their supervisor, which they often cover); Show all lines for the others.
  2. Tap the situation
    The situation decides the problem type, the department and the people alerted — automatically. Several situations can be combined.
  3. Tap ANDON CALL
    The bar shows who gets it (e.g. “FRT · equipment failure → MT 3”). When the ANDON number appears, it is on the dashboard and the people are alerted.
  4. Wait for ‘acknowledged’
    The caller is told when a responder acknowledges (MT: ‘repair started’) and when it is closed.

5. Respond

  1. Open the ANDON from the alert
    Or from Respond on the home screen.
  2. ACKNOWLEDGE — “I am on my way”
    Red turns amber on the dashboard and the caller is told. Android / PC Chrome: the alert itself has an Acknowledge button (MT: Start repair).
  3. ACTION — record what is being done (optional)
  4. CLOSE — enter the corrective action and save
    The line turns green for 24 hours, then the ANDON stays in the history.

6. Dashboard · history

Live dashboard — the plant map: a line with a problem lights up where it is. The name of the person who acknowledged / closed is shown (never contact data).

The ‘ACTIVE ANDON’ list on the right shows the elapsed time, oldest first; tap a tile or an entry for the details. History shows counts, average response and resolution times, by line and type, repeated issues and the latest 200 calls.

History is kept for 1 year. Save as CSV on the History screen exports a period (opens in Excel).
Dashboard owner · installer

7. Big-screen dashboard PC

  1. Chrome · 1920 × 1080
    Install Chrome; display resolution 1920 × 1080 (4K screen: scale 200 %).
  2. Kiosk shortcut
    Chrome shortcut → Properties → at the end of ‘Target’ add
    --kiosk https://forvia-yeongcheon-andon.vercel.app/dashboard
  3. Log in once with the dashboard account
    Plant data needs a login. The view-only big-screen account stays logged in while the screen is on.
  4. Turn on live updates
    Click the yellow Turn on live updates once and allow notifications: the board then changes within seconds of every call (silent push, no polling). Keep the window visible.
  5. Auto start · no sleep · power-on after power loss
    Win + R → shell:startup → copy the shortcut there; power settings: screen off / sleep never; BIOS: Restore on AC power loss.
  6. LTE data
    Set the LTE router connection as metered in Windows; schedule Windows Update restarts outside working hours.
Keep the dashboard on. The 2-hour escalation is checked by the server every minute, even when the dashboard is off. Green ● LIVE = connected; a red warning appears when the connection is lost. Alt + F4 closes the kiosk for maintenance.

8. FAQ

“Cannot call ANDON” on the call screen

Only GAP leaders and supervisors can call. Visitor accounts are view-only.

No acknowledge button on an ANDON

Only the called department can act on it; other accounts can only view.

“Connection lost” at the top

Check the network; the screen updates by itself when it is back.

Forgot the password

Login screen → ‘Forgot your password?’ (needs a linked KakaoTalk), or ask the system owner.

Company PC shows a ‘Cloud Auth’ page first

That is the FORVIA network security sign-in, not ANDON. After it, the ANDON login appears.
